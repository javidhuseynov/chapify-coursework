Questions

Which files can I print?
PDF files up to {{ max_size_mb }} MB and {{ max_pages }} pages that are not password-protected. In Word or Google Docs, choose Save as PDF or Download as PDF first.
Why can't I change the number of pages?
Chapify counts the pages in your PDF so that the price is always exact.
When does printing start?
As soon as the payment provider confirms your payment, your job joins the print queue. Follow it on the request page.
My payment was declined. What now?
Open the request and choose Try payment again, or change the settings first. Nothing is charged for a declined payment.
My print job failed.
Staff are notified on their dashboard and can send the job to the printer again. Your file is kept for {{ retention_hours }} hours for this.
I can't log in any more.
After {{ login_limit }} wrong passwords, logging in from your computer is paused for {{ lockout_minutes }} minutes. If your account was blocked, contact the library desk.

Privacy and your documents

  • Only you and the service staff can see your requests and payments.
  • Your PDF is deleted as soon as it has been printed or the request is cancelled. Unpaid requests are cancelled after {{ retention_hours }} hours and their files deleted.
  • Chapify never receives your card details; payments are handled by the payment provider.
  • For security, logins and important actions are recorded in an audit log that only staff can view.
